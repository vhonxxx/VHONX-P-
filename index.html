A Letter from Mom

I crouched beside where you lay, cradling your head in my arms, looking out the window through tears and the beeping of machines around us, In such a beautiful starry night I realized, from that moment I saw how the shape of your eyes were like mine, the curve of your nose, the slight dimple on your cheek when you smile. But soon i came to, your eyes remained closed, your breathing shallow, your hands resembled your little ones back then as you gripped weakly on my mine,as if telling me"It’s time for me to go..i’m not afraid anymore…everything will be alright now." , nurses took you from my arms, rushing, scuttling, trying everything they can to get you back to me but your eyes remained closed, your breath non existent, your hands never squeezed mine back as I squeezed yours. my darling, you were the star that ignited the dark parts of my life, but it gradually dimmed as you lived on; and on this starry night , you took your life. I'm thankful that you were a part of this life , starry night , a part of me, a part of everyone that appreciate the light you shine, and now, you shine brighter; as a star above, joining this night's sky. -mom
